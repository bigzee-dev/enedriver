### Create a services component

Create a "Our Services" component that will go into the homepage. Place it after the <WhyChooseUs /> component. The "Our services" component will display the services offered by the company. Follow all project conventions in CLAUDE.md

## services

-Driver hire Agency
-Commercial vehicle deliveries and collection local and international
-Commercial vehicle registration & Transport tests appointments & Bookings

## look and design

Have a title at the top of the component, align it in the middle. There are 3 services so there will be 3 cards. Use .map to output the cards. Let a card have an image taking up the full height of the card. You can use any of the image in the /public folder. Have an overlay over the image starting off transparent at the top and getting dark as it goes to the bottom. At the botttom have the title of the service and a small description.
